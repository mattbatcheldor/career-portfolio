Inside the platform

I didn't discover YouTube during the creator boom. I watched it become a business.

At Google in the UK and New Zealand, I worked with major advertisers and their agencies as online video moved from experimental digital inventory to a serious part of brand and commercial strategy. The work was never simply selling YouTube. It was helping marketers decide whether video deserved real budget, and proving it when it did.

Audience system

Discovery is not the same as audience.

A view is discovery. An audience is what happens when that view turns into depth, return visits and trust. The useful commercial question is not simply how many people saw something, but whether discovery compounds into a relationship.

Creator economics

Creator control + Audience trust + Commercial alignment = Sustainable value.

The weaker question is “How do we monetise this audience?” The better question is how to create commercial value without destroying the trust that created the value in the first place.

Measurement

What evidence should change the next decision?

Across brand lift, enterprise growth, owned audience development, creator campaigns and AI experiments, the measurement question has remained consistent: evidence matters when it changes what you do next.

Discovery → depth

Separate initial reach from meaningful audience behaviour.

Paid → organic

Understand what distribution moved and what the audience chose to continue.

Experiment → decision

Use evidence to decide what deserves the next investment.

AI → judgement

Use AI to improve decision quality, with human judgement retained at the centre.

The proposition

I am not every specialist in this ecosystem. I have learned how to connect them.

The executive skill in the middle is orchestration: understanding each party's incentives, connecting specialists around one outcome, recognising the opportunity early, building partnerships that last, turning audience into sustainable commercial value, and letting evidence decide the next move.

The next chapter of the creator economy will not be won by content alone.

It will be built at the intersection of creator trust, audience behaviour, platform technology, measurement, commercial partnerships and AI.

I have spent my career learning how those systems connect. That is the work I want to continue.
