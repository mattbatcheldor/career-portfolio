SaaS Growth Case Study · Google × Vend · 2014-15

Scaling customer acquisition for a global retail SaaS platform

How a fast-growing New Zealand cloud software company used search, funnel design, market prioritisation and measurement to build a more scalable acquisition engine.

Matthew BatcheldorIndustry Head, Large Customer Sales, Google New Zealand
01 · The business

Vend brought SaaS to physical retail

Cloud-native retail platform

Point of sale, inventory, reporting and customer management in the cloud, later extending into ecommerce and omnichannel retail.

Global from New Zealand

Founded in Auckland, Vend was already serving retailers across Australia, North America, the UK and other markets by 2014-15.

A true SaaS growth model

Growth was measured in leads, free trials, activation, paying customers and monthly recurring revenue, not one-off transactions.

02 · The growth problem

Traffic was growing. The engine needed to mature.

5,500trial accounts per month in the 2014-15 working baseline
25-30%of monthly trial accounts came from Google
Globalmarket-by-market acquisition economics
MQL → MRRfocus beyond raw trial volume

The strategic shift was from maximising clicks and trials to improving the quality and economics of the entire SaaS revenue cycle.

03 · SaaS growth framework

From clicks to the full SaaS revenue cycle

AwarenessLeadTrialActivationPaid customerMRR

Journey design

Map visitors from anonymous through engaged lead, sales lead, opportunity and won, then match content and conversion paths to buying intent.

Measurement

Connect campaign activity to downstream return on investment through Marketo and improve data accuracy across lead types and markets.

Conversion

Reduce signup friction, introduce differentiated landing paths and make conversion rate optimisation an ongoing discipline.

04 · Global GTM

Global growth as a portfolio of market economics

Australia14.3% spend · 24.6% leads · highest activation ratio
UK20.0% spend · 18.3% leads · local investment supporting growth
North America38.5% spend · 25.9% leads · Search CPA $60 / Display CPA $90
Rest of world3.4% spend · 6.2% leads · low CPA and good activation

Budget decisions were made market by market, reflecting different acquisition costs, lead returns and activation performance.

05 · Operating system

A more mature acquisition engine

Campaign architecture

Tighter ad groups, vertical segmentation, geographic and time-of-day optimisation, backed by disciplined A/B testing.

Mobile and video

A dedicated mobile strategy, video campaigns and tests designed to drive app installs and usage.

Landing-page optimisation

A move toward one-step signup, continuous landing-page testing and separate conversion paths for different lead types.

Lifecycle marketing

Beyond trial signups into pre-trial leads, newsletters, ebooks and webinars, each nurtured by buying stage.

06 · Why this matters

A growth partnership across the whole SaaS funnel

Commercial problem-solving

Linked acquisition spend to trials, activation, recurring revenue and expansion into new markets.

Cross-functional SaaS thinking

Covered customer journey, lifecycle, conversion, analytics, mobile, content, display, video and market prioritisation.

Repeatable SaaS experience

Alongside Xero, Vend was a second direct SaaS client relationship in my Google career, with a different product, buyer and growth challenge.

The work was about helping a young SaaS company decide where each dollar of acquisition would create the most recurring revenue, one market at a time.

Vend later became part of Lightspeed. This historical case study shows the nature and breadth of the work in 2014-15. All metrics are historical and time-bound.
