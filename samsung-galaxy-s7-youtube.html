Partnership Case Study · New Zealand, 2016

Samsung Galaxy S7 × YouTube

A local film, live measurement and one joined-up partnership that helped Samsung take the top spot in New Zealand smartphones.

+24%lift in ad recall, target audience

01 · The challenge

Most Kiwis already owned a smartphone. Samsung needed them to look up.

People were largely happy with the phone in their pocket. Reach alone would not move them. The launch needed a story young New Zealanders would recognise as their own, built around the Galaxy S7's water resistance and New Zealand's everyday water culture.

02 · The partnership

Four organisations, one shared scorecard.

Brand, creative, media and platform each owned a piece of the result. The work was in keeping everyone looking at the same data and moving in the same direction.

Samsung New Zealand

Set the ambition: a distinctive, local brand story for the S7 launch.

Colenso BBDO

Created “Now You Can”, a short film built around life on and in the water.

Starcom Worldwide

Media partner on the buy, tuning targeting and ad length across later flights.

Google / YouTube

TrueView media, near real-time analytics and a Brand Lift study to steer optimisation.

03 · How we ran it

Measure while it is live, improve every flight.

1 · Give the story roomYouTube freed the brand from the limits of a standard TV spot, allowing the film to run close to two minutes.
2 · Test the edit in marketSkippable TrueView enabled different ad lengths to be trialled while the campaign was running.
3 · Measure what people rememberA Brand Lift study tracked impact on the target audience and flagged media and creative fixes.
4 · Carry the learning forwardNear real-time demographic and view-through data shaped each additional flight and future content.
04 · The outcome

People watched, remembered, and bought.

+24%lift in ad recall among young New Zealanders
3.4×more cost efficient on a completed-view basis
#1in New Zealand smartphones, held for more consecutive months in 2016 than any prior year
500Kviews in under two months
77%of the near two-minute film watched on average
~24%view-through rate

What it shows: Bringing a client, two agencies and a platform onto one measurement plan, then using live data to make the creative and media better while money was still being spent.

Source: Think with Google APAC, “Samsung’s Mike Cornwell on How the Brand Made a Splash on YouTube”, January 2017. Results shown in original campaign context.
